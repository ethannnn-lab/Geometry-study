# Geometry Study

A study site for Unit 2 (parallelograms) and the June 23, 2026 NYS Geometry Regents.

Open `index.html` in any browser. Modes:

- **Parallelogram Quiz**: Amplify Unit 2 (Go With the Flow, A-Door-Able Parallelograms)
- **Congruence & Moves**: Amplify practice on SSS/SAS/ASA, congruence statements and transformations
- **Regents Part I**: all 24 multiple-choice questions from the June 23, 2026 exam
- **Regents Parts II–IV**: questions 25–35 with answer checking and worked solutions
- **Proof Builder**: pick the reason for each step of a two-column proof
- **Flashcards**: vocab and theorems
- **Speed Round**: 60 seconds of true/false
- **Mixed Review**: random questions from everything

Part I answers match the official NYSED scoring key for June 2026.

Points and best streak are saved in your browser.
