# Geometry Study

A study site for Unit 2 (parallelograms) and the June 23, 2026 NYS Geometry Regents.

Open `index.html` in any browser. Modes:

- **Parallelogram Quiz**: angles, diagonals, coordinates and proof reasons
- **Regents Practice**: the June 2026 exam questions plus Regents-style practice
- **Proof Builder**: pick the reason for each step of a two-column proof
- **Flashcards**: vocab and theorems
- **Speed Round**: 60 seconds of true/false
- **Mixed Review**: random questions from everything

Points and best streak are saved in your browser.
